Welcome.

This site is less a blog and more a **journal** — a place to write down the things
worth revising. When I learn or re-learn something, I note it here in plain language so
it's quick to get back up to speed later, without digging through scattered bookmarks.

If a post helps you too, that's a bonus. Expect notes that are practical, example-driven,
and revisited whenever there's a better way to explain them.
