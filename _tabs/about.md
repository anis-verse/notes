Welcome.

This site is part **blog**, part **reference journal** — a place to write down the
things worth revising. Some entries read like walkthroughs you follow start to finish;
others are notes I come back to when I need to look something up. When I learn or
re-learn something, I write it down in plain language so it's quick to get back up to
speed later, without digging through scattered bookmarks.

If a post helps you too, that's a bonus. Expect content that's practical, example-driven,
and revisited whenever there's a better way to explain it.

> Some posts here are co-authored with AI assistants such as Claude Code. I use them to
> draft, structure, and cross-check technical detail, but every post is reviewed, edited,
> and verified by me before it's published — the judgement and the mistakes are my own.
